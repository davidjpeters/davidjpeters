I enjoy a lot of things, namely programming, video games, music and creative outlets. Problem solving and creative ideas are what get me excited about coding, and I love opportunities to tackle unique problems or ideas with new tools and stacks

#### Work
Student Bioinformatics Programmer at Agriculture and Agri-Food Canada (Jan 2026 - Current) working on developing and deploying science applications onto the Red Hat OpenShift platform and integrating these tools with the Government of Canada's high-performance computing cluster

#### Research 
Exploring novel local search decomposition techniques on large, non-deterministic optimization problems for both AC and DC Multi-Period Optimal Power Flow problems

Repository can be found [here](https://github.com/Lethbridge-Optimization-Research-Group/power)

Publications:

- [Enhancing Optimal Power Flow Feasibility: A Novel Comparative Study of Approximation Techniques](https://utc.hal.science/hal-05085075v1)
